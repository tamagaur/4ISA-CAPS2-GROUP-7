## Populations

- **forecastable**: Products with a scored month in the window, at least one month with sales above 0, and no near-zero month (sales above 0 but under 5% of the product's usual monthly level, accuracy_mart.avg_monthly_pieces, the mean of the 12 months before; backend/marts.NEAR_ZERO_SHARE). The error card's MAPE population.
- **order plan**: The forecastable products with a row in the current order plan (backend/lp_cycle.PLAN_STATUS_SQL, Unilever, the latest cycle). The error card's planned-products line. Backtest errors on past months, not the plan's quantities.
- **all scored**: Every product with a scored month in the window, the near-zero products and those that sold nothing there included. The error card's two WAPEs; over the full span, the published headline's 310 products.

## Definitions

- **MAE**: mean over the population's product-months of |forecast - actual|, in pieces.
- **RMSE**: square root of the mean over the population's product-months of (forecast - actual)^2, in pieces.
- **WAPE**: sum over the population's product-months of |forecast - actual| / sum of actual (pooled; bigger sellers weigh more).
- **MAPE**: median over the population's products of each product's mean |forecast - actual| / actual over its months with actual > 0 (months with actual = 0 have no percentage and are left out; the product count is the products with at least one such month).
- **MASE**: median over the population's products of (the product's MAE over its scored months in the window) / (the mean |change from one month to the next| of the product's own demand series over its months BEFORE the window: the in-sample error of repeating last month). Demand series: Unilever_SKU_Demand_Fact_Real by product and month from the first month with a known quantity, a month with no row is 0 sold (the Unilever rule), a blank capture is unknown and pairs with it are skipped, negative months count as 0. A product with no such pair before the window, or a scale of 0, has no MASE and is not counted. Below 1 = better than repeating last month. NOT the stored MASE of Forecast_Result (the plan's entry test: the served model's rolling one-step error over the holdout, the last 20% of each product's history, on the latest forecast only; backend/routers/dashboard.ENTRY_TEST_MEASURE, docs/PUBLISHED_FIGURES_SNAPSHOT_2026-10-24.json 'mase').
- **R²**: median over the population's products of 1 - sum (actual - forecast)^2 / sum (actual - benchmark)^2 over the product's scored months that have a benchmark, where the benchmark of month t is the mean of the product's demand series over every known month BEFORE t (an expanding mean, knowable when the forecast was made). Products with fewer than 3 such months, or a denominator of 0, have no R² and are not counted. Above 0 = the forecast beats the product's own past average. The mean of the scored months themselves is never the benchmark (hindsight).

## Context figures

- **mean_actual_pieces**: mean actual pieces per product-month (MAE and RMSE read against this).
- **MAPE_mean**: the mean instead of the median of the same per-product percentages.
- **MASE_share_below_1**: share of the MASE products with MASE below 1.
- **R2_share_above_0**: share of the R² products with R² above 0.
- **MASE_mean**: the mean instead of the median of the same per-product MASEs (every product with a MASE in the population).
- **MASE_mean_without_sku**: the same mean without SKU 68763994; MASE_mean_without_sku_products is its product count.
- **R2_mean**: the mean instead of the median of the same per-product R²s. A mean follows the worst product: over the full span one R² far below the rest (SKU 68763994) moves it where the median does not; in the card window that product has no R² (it is not in the set), so the two means agree there.
- **R2_mean_without_sku**: the same mean without SKU 68763994; R2_mean_without_sku_products is its product count.
