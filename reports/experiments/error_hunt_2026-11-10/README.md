# Error hunt, 2026-11-10

Brief (Kelvin, 2026-11-10): lower forecast error by any means, with nothing in the design protected. Two rules: nothing is
written to live without his go, and everything is scored in both windows. BACKTEST ONLY. The scripts read the throwaway
copy `ssm_challenger` through `../error_reduction_2026-11-10/lab.py` (READ ONLY; it refuses any other server). Per-product
rows go to the git-ignored `local/` (hard rule 9). Live was read three times, each a `run_lp --dry-run` (one READ ONLY
transaction, no write): with the switch off it reproduced lp_run 7 exactly (110 rows, 419,344 pieces).

**Scoring.** The frame is the 4,370 as-served product-months, 2024-01..2026-02. The base is S1: the forecast served since
2026-11-10 (router, calendar factor, succession, fading). Every candidate uses only months before the one it forecasts.
Pooled WAPE and MASE lead, with MAPE in `results_*.csv`. The MASE scale is fixed per product on 2023-01..2025-08, the same
in both windows. The windows are main (2025-09..2026-02) and earlier (2024-01..2025-08). The sets are all products (208 in
main, 270 in earlier) and planned products (lp_run 7's 110). December and January have their own lines in the CSVs. The gate is
lab.py's, fixed before any of this was scored: lower pooled WAPE in both windows, a 95% product-bootstrap interval below 0 in
at least one, and MASE not higher in either.

## What the numbers say

1. **The monthly product error is close to its floor for any forecast built on the level.** Oracles, which are not forecasts:
   - Knowing each product's true average level over the scored window: main 34.53% (−3.53 pts), earlier 26.79% (−1.07).
   - Knowing the company's true level each month: 35.76% (−2.31) and 24.01% (−3.85).
   - A centred 3-month mean that includes the month itself: 32.42% and 18.93%.

   About 30 to 34 points of the 38% main-window WAPE are month-to-month swing that no level forecast can contain.
   S1 is within about 3.5 points of a perfect level.
2. **The unit is not the lever.** On the same rows S1 scores:
   - Per product-month: 37.9% in pieces and 40.8% in pesos.
   - On monthly company totals: 11.7% in pieces and 12.0% in pesos (main window).

   The 37.88% vs 10.77% gap in `docs/ACCURACY_AS_SERVED.md` is aggregation, not the unit: the peso figure is
   the WAPE of company totals (`scripts/accuracy_as_served.py:374`), where products' errors cancel. Price per piece is
   flat within a code (median CV 0.9%), so a peso forecast converted back to pieces is the piece forecast (`peso_ma3` and
   `ma3` differ by 0.13 pts).
3. **The error falls with the review period.** For S1, pooled WAPE is 38.1% over 1 month, 26.6% over 2 months and 19.7%
   over 3 months (main window). In the earlier window it is 27.9%, 18.7% and 17.1%. At flavour-group level per month it is
   32.3% and 25.2%. These are not the same target as the monthly table: they say how much of the error is timing inside a
   longer order cycle. That is an architecture point (monthly product grain), not a forecast change.
4. **Sell-out (Unilever): blocked.** `sellout_fact` has 0 Unilever rows and `data/raw/Unilever` has no sell-out file. Only
   Del Monte has sell-out.
5. **Promotions: no signal.** No Unilever pricelist exists. The only promo pricelist in the inventory is a Del Monte PDF
   (October 2023), and it is not on disk. Net over gross per code is flat (within-code standard deviation 1.1 pts), so
   price carries no promo timing. Promo or bundle sibling codes selling: −0.3% median effect on the base code, and −3.4%
   in the sibling's first month. Both `promo_lag` and `promo_cal` (the second assumes a promo calendar is known) are
   inside ±0.11 pts.
