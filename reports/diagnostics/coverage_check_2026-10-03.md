## Legend

**R** real (`is_demo = false`) · **G** generated by the synthetic v2 generator (`fill_source = 'synthetic_v2'`) ·
**F·gap** `synthetic_v2_gap` · **F·rate** `synthetic_rate_transfer` · **F·split** `synthetic_category_split` ·
**F·derived** `derived_stock_balance` · **F·branch** `synthetic_branch_adjacent` (a branch's adjacent real month, copied; H2) ·
**—** MISSING (no rows) · **n/a** the table cannot hold this principal · ⚑ real but a branch's real rows are missing.
Numbers are row counts.

**GENERATED is not only 2021-22.** The synthetic v2 generator's rows (tagged `synthetic_v2` by migration 0038) also fill every
interior month with no real data, e.g. Del Monte sales 2024-03..2025-02, Del Monte purchases 2021-01..2023-08, and Unilever sales
2026-03..2026-08. The DEC-31 fill step only filled cells that were still empty after that.

Data types → tables. Del Monte: sales `sales_fact` · sell-out `sellout_fact` · purchases `purchase_fact` · returns
`salesreturn_fact` · stock `inventory_fact` · product sales `sku_demand_fill`. Unilever: sales `unilever_sku_demand_fact`, which is
already product (SKU) × month, so **product-level sales are the same rows**; purchases `purchase_fact` (0 rows) +
`derived_receipt_fact`; returns `salesreturn_fact`; stock `inventory_fact`; sell-out n/a (the table's CHECK allows Del Monte only;
0 Unilever rows). Month = `_period` (`calendar_month` for Unilever sales). Cross-check: every cell's status agrees with
`etl_coverage` (0 mismatches).
