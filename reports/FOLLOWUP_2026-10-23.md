### 3c. Does the 2 March count balance?

**Plain answer: it cannot be made to balance or to fail, because Unilever has no receipts.** `purchase_fact_real` holds Del Monte only (499 rows, 0 Unilever) and `po_qty_*` is empty in every Unilever count (0 of 5 recent counts carry it). The only Unilever "receipts" in the database are `derived_receipt_fact`, which is *defined* as closing − opening + sold, so the identity holds for any count by construction. What can be tested is whether the receipts it implies are possible and ordinary. Sales are per-SKU monthly sales (`unilever_sku_demand_fact_real`, taken as pieces as DEC-31 does); they end at 2026-02, **there is no March sales figure at all.**

Stock moves are checked as previous count + receipts − sales = next count, with receipts as the unknown. Unilever, totals in pieces; "gap" is the part that cannot be receipts (a product whose stock fell by more than it sold, which would need negative receipts).

Against the other 36 months (2023-02 .. 2026-01): implied receipts median 2,787,092, range 514,530 – 5,309,580; receipts/sales median 0.99, 90th percentile 1.71. **February 2026's 3,827,499 is the 5th highest of 37 months; its ratio of 2.17 is the 2nd highest.** A total-level gap of −38,417 pieces (1.0% of the count) is small beside the other 36 months (median 5.7% of the closing count, maximum 74.8%; only 2025-06, at 0.4%, is smaller among the 14 most recent).

**2 Mar → 6 Apr (the "March to April" step).** With receipts ≥ 0 the March count is possible only if March sales were at least Σ max(0, Feb − Mar) over products = **2,202,506 pieces** (the 12-month average month is 2,330,836; the largest month in 37 is 3,575,019) and next to nothing was received. Months that followed a high count did show receipts/sales of 0.24, 0.31 and 0.29 (2025-02, 2025-08, 2026-01); March would need about 0.09 at an average sales month, or 0.4 at the largest month on record.

**What this decides.** The 2 March count is not shown to be wrong: its total is possible, similar spikes followed by a drop occurred in 2025-01, 2025-06 and 2025-12, and the stock is concentrated in fast-moving powders that plausibly arrived in bulk. It is not shown to be right either, for three reasons it cannot rule out: (1) 3.83M pieces received in one month is 2.17× that month's sales and nobody has the delivery records; (2) the next count only works if March received almost nothing, and 189 products fall further than they ever sold in a month; (3) the stock file is the Ilocos Sur warehouse and the per-product sales are La Union only (UNVERIFIED, recorded in `lp_model.inputs.notes`), so the sales side of every balance here is incomplete by an unknown amount. **The deciding evidence is SSM's Feb 2026 delivery receipts (about 3.83M pieces) or confirmation of what left the warehouse in March; neither is in the database.** The plan (109 rows, 435,988 pieces; 36 rows plan zero) is built on this count and was not rebuilt.
