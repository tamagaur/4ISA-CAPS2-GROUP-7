### 3c. Does the 2 March count balance?

**Plain answer: it cannot be made to balance or to fail, because Unilever has no receipts.** `purchase_fact_real` holds Del Monte only (499 rows, 0 Unilever) and `po_qty_*` is empty in every Unilever count (0 of 5 recent counts carry it). The only Unilever "receipts" in the database are `derived_receipt_fact`, which is *defined* as closing − opening + sold, so the identity holds for any count by construction. What can be tested is whether the receipts it implies are possible and ordinary. Sales are per-SKU monthly sales (`unilever_sku_demand_fact_real`, taken as pieces as DEC-31 does); they end at 2026-02, **there is no March sales figure at all.**

Stock moves are checked as previous count + receipts − sales = next count, with receipts as the unknown. Unilever, totals in pieces; "gap" is the part that cannot be receipts (a product whose stock fell by more than it sold, which would need negative receipts).

| Count (file month, counted) | Previous count | Sales in between | Implied receipts | Receipts / sales | Negative-receipt gap (products) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025-12 (1 Jan 2026) | 2,609,809 → 3,541,522 | 1,349,085 | 2,280,798 | 1.69 | −151,690 (4.3% of closing; 282 products) |
| 2026-01 (3 Feb 2026) | 3,542,859 → 1,735,540 | 2,559,657 | 752,338 | 0.29 | −715,232 (41.2%; 278) |
| **2026-02 (2 Mar 2026)** | **1,735,538 → 3,800,930** | **1,762,107** | **3,827,499** | **2.17** | **−38,417 (1.0%; 175)** |
| 2026-03 (6 Apr 2026) | 3,800,930 → 1,685,621 | **not in the data** | needs sales ≥ 2,115,309 net | — | see below |

Against the other 36 months (2023-02 .. 2026-01): implied receipts median 2,787,092, range 514,530 – 5,309,580; receipts/sales median 0.99, 90th percentile 1.71. **February 2026's 3,827,499 is the 5th highest of 37 months; its ratio of 2.17 is the 2nd highest.** A total-level gap of −38,417 pieces (1.0% of the count) is small beside the other 36 months (median 5.7% of the closing count, maximum 74.8%; only 2025-06, at 0.4%, is smaller among the 14 most recent).

**2 Mar → 6 Apr (the "March to April" step).** With receipts ≥ 0 the March count is possible only if March sales were at least Σ max(0, Feb − Mar) over products = **2,202,506 pieces** (the 12-month average month is 2,330,836; the largest month in 37 is 3,575,019) and next to nothing was received. Months that followed a high count did show receipts/sales of 0.24, 0.31 and 0.29 (2025-02, 2025-08, 2026-01); March would need about 0.09 at an average sales month, or 0.4 at the largest month on record.

**What this decides.** The 2 March count is not shown to be wrong: its total is possible, similar spikes followed by a drop occurred in 2025-01, 2025-06 and 2025-12, and the stock is concentrated in fast-moving powders that plausibly arrived in bulk. It is not shown to be right either, for three reasons it cannot rule out: (1) 3.83M pieces received in one month is 2.17× that month's sales and nobody has the delivery records; (2) the next count only works if March received almost nothing, and 189 products fall further than they ever sold in a month; (3) the stock file is the Ilocos Sur warehouse and the per-product sales are La Union only (UNVERIFIED, recorded in `lp_model.inputs.notes`), so the sales side of every balance here is incomplete by an unknown amount. **The deciding evidence is SSM's Feb 2026 delivery receipts (about 3.83M pieces) or confirmation of what left the warehouse in March; neither is in the database.** The plan (109 rows, 435,988 pieces; 36 rows plan zero) is built on this count and was not rebuilt.

## Part 6: what it would take for the planning period to advance a month (scoping only, nothing built)

**Confirmed: an accepted recommendation feeds nothing.** Searched `analytics/`, `scripts/` and `backend/` on 2026-10-23: `'accepted'` is read only by the decision endpoints, `lp_cycle`/`planning_mart` (which keep a decided row in the plan) and the model's CHECK. `scripts/run_lp.py` takes stock from `inventory_fact_real` at the opening count and nothing else; `decided_q_i` is capture only by rule (a test fails if anything outside the endpoint reads it); PO Qty is shown, never subtracted (DEC-34), and the PO Qty columns are empty in every recent Unilever count. The next plan can only learn that something was bought by finding it in the next stock count.

**a. Recording an accepted order as expected stock.**

| Needed | Why / what exists |
|---|---|
| A table of expected receipts (new migration, next number 0069): recommendation_key, product, pieces, ordered_at, expected_on, state (`expected` / `arrived` / `cancelled`) | Nothing holds this today. Pieces = `COALESCE(decided_q_i, q_i)`, the only place the planner's number lives, so the capture-only rule has to be lifted by decision, with its test changed on purpose. |
| Written in the accept transaction (`decide_row`), voided in the reversal (`/reverse`) and on a dismissal | Those are the only writers of a decision; the planning mart already follows them in one transaction. |
| `expected_on` = ordered_at + lead time | `Product_Dimension.lead_time_days` (migration 0045) and `PLACEHOLDERS["lead_days"]` exist; both are placeholders, SSM has not given a lead time. |
| What decides it arrived | Not in the data. The only evidence is the next count: an order counts as arrived when a stock count dated on or after `expected_on` exists, and is then inside that count (not added again). Unilever has no delivery records (finding in Part 3), so this is an assumption, not a measurement. Whatever is still `expected` at a cycle's planning would be subtracted from that cycle's need (this is the "net of stock" gap already on record). |

**c. If a historical month is corrected after accuracy figures were published from it.** The stored forecast side is frozen (`mart.forecast_run_value`, "never rebuilt"), but `mart.accuracy_mart` reads the actuals from the live sales mart at every refresh, and the loader refreshes the marts. So the published accuracy (`docs/ACCURACY_AS_SERVED.md`, the Dashboard's accuracy card, the error decomposition) **changes silently** the next time anything loads, with no record that the actuals changed, no version stamp on the document, and no flag on the card. Everything else built from that month is stale and nothing says so: the stored Forecast_Result rows (MA3 and the model routing use the old actuals), ABC tiers and the `t12m_new` window for that period, segmentation bins and traits, review flags, the dead-SKU rule, the calendar factor, safety stock (its sigma is the history), and the plan. `planning_stale` (migrations 0047, 0061) notices a changed Tier A basis or review flags, not changed actuals. What exists to build on: `etl_load_event` and `etl_cutover_ledger` record each slice load. What a correction process needs: a change of actuals marks every dependent output stale, and a published figure carries the actuals version it was computed on.
